Most dogs don't sleep on the sofa because they love the sofa. They sleep there
because it's soft, it's where you are, and nothing else in the room is competing.

its own — no training, no telling anyone off.

That's really all a dog corner is: **the right spot, made comfortable enough to
win.** Here are 12 ideas that work in a normal-sized home, including the ones
that fix the two problems nobody warns you about — the mess and the smell.

## 1. Put it where your dog already lies down

Before buying anything, watch where your dog naps for two days. Almost every dog
already has a favourite patch — beside the sofa, in a doorway with a view of the
room, near a radiator. **Build the corner there.** A perfect setup in the wrong
place gets ignored, and a plain blanket in the right place gets used every night.

## 2. Use a real corner, not the middle of a wall

A right angle does half the work for you: two walls mean two sides your dog can
lean against, and it keeps the bed out of the walking line between rooms. It's
also the bit of floor you were least likely to use for anything else, which is
why a dog corner rarely costs you any real space.

## 3. Start with a bed that has a rim

A

is the single upgrade that changes behaviour. Dogs burrow into sofa cushions for
the edge, not the softness, and a raised rim gives them that edge in a bed they're
allowed to have. Size it so they can curl up with a little room spare, not stretch
out flat.

## 4. Give the toys one basket

Dog toys are the reason a dog corner looks messy rather than cosy. A single
[woven storage basket](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=woven+dog+toy+storage+basket&tag=tamirugc20-20)
sat next to the bed turns clean-up into one sweep of your arm, and most dogs learn
within a week that the basket is where the toys live — several will start fetching
from it themselves.

## 5. Add a washable blanket on top

This is the trick that keeps the room liveable. Put a
[washable dog blanket](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washable+dog+blanket+for+furniture&tag=tamirugc20-20)
over the bed and wash *that* every week instead of wrestling the bed into the
machine. It's the difference between a corner you keep fresh and one you slowly
stop noticing has gone stale.

## 6. Put a rug under the whole thing

A small
[washable area rug](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washable+small+area+rug&tag=tamirugc20-20)
under the bed and basket does two jobs: it visually frames the corner so it reads
as a deliberate part of the room rather than dog stuff dumped on the floor, and it
catches most of the hair and grit before it travels. Choose one you can put in a
machine, not a beautiful one you'll be scared of.

## 7. Keep water there, not in the kitchen

If the water bowl lives two rooms away, your dog spends the evening walking back
and forth past the sofa. A
[spill-proof water bowl](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=spill+proof+dog+water+bowl&tag=tamirugc20-20)
in the corner keeps everything they need in one place — which is a surprisingly
large part of why they settle there.

## 8. Give them a view

Dogs choose a spot by what they can see. A corner facing the door or the main
walkway will beat a quieter, comfier one in a dead-end nook nearly every time,
because the whole point of the sofa was watching the room. If your only good
corner faces a wall, angle the bed outward.

## 9. Protect the wall behind it

The wall beside a dog bed picks up nose prints, fur and the occasional muddy
shake. A
[wall-mounted leash and towel hook](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=wall+mounted+dog+leash+hook&tag=tamirugc20-20)
plus a small mat there gives the corner a job beyond sleeping — it becomes the
place the lead, the towel and the harness live, so the whole dog operation stops
spreading through the house.

## 10. Use a low crate as furniture

If you already have a crate, a
[crate cover or topper](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=dog+crate+cover+wooden+top&tag=tamirugc20-20)
turns it into a side table with a den underneath. Doors open, blanket inside,
lamp on top — it stops looking like equipment and starts looking like the corner
was designed that way.

## 11. Add one soft light

A dog corner in a dark corner feels like a punishment corner. A small
[warm-white table lamp](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=small+warm+white+table+lamp&tag=tamirugc20-20)
nearby makes the whole area read as part of the cosy room in the evening — the
same trick that makes a
[reading nook](/cozy-reading-nook-ideas/) work, applied a metre lower.

## 12. Leave something of yours in it

The unglamorous last step: put an old jumper or a t-shirt you've worn in the bed
for the first week. Dogs settle on your smell, and this is usually what tips a
dog who "won't use the new bed" into using it by the third night. Wash it out
once the habit is set.

## How to set it up in one afternoon

Pick the spot your dog already uses. Lay a washable rug. Put a rimmed bed on it,
angled to face the room. Add a basket for toys, a water bowl, a blanket over the
bed and a worn t-shirt inside it. Hang a hook on the wall for the lead. That's
the whole build, and it's an hour of work — the only part that takes patience is
letting your dog decide it's theirs, which usually takes about three nights.

## Common questions

### How do I get my dog to use their new bed instead of the sofa?

Put the bed where they already choose to lie, add something that smells like you,
and make it the softer of the two options with a
[rimmed donut bed](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=donut+calming+dog+bed&tag=tamirugc20-20).
Most dogs switch within a week. If they don't, the location is usually wrong
rather than the bed.

### Where is the best place for a dog corner in a small home?

Beside the sofa, out of the walking line, with a view of the door. Corners work
best because they give two leaning walls and use floor you weren't using anyway.
Our [best dog beds for small spaces](/best-dog-beds-small-spaces/) guide covers
which bed shapes fit the tightest gaps.

### How do I stop the dog corner from smelling?

Wash a
[blanket](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washable+dog+blanket+for+furniture&tag=tamirugc20-20)
laid over the bed weekly rather than washing the bed itself, keep a washable rug
underneath, and choose a bed with a zip-off cover. The smell comes from padding
that never gets cleaned, so the goal is to keep the dirt on the layers you *can*
clean easily.

### Do dogs actually need their own space?

Most do settle better with one. A defined spot gives a dog somewhere to go when
the house is busy, which is why so many stop pacing in the evening once they have
one. It doesn't have to be big — a bed, a blanket and a corner is enough.
