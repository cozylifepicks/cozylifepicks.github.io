floor is already spoken for, so anything new has to hang, stack or hook onto
something that's currently doing nothing. That's the whole trick, and it's why
half the "small bathroom storage" you can buy is useless — it wants floor you
haven't got.

We compared the storage that works in a genuinely tiny bathroom, sorted by which
piece of wasted space it claims back. Here are the ten worth your money.

## 4. Suction or adhesive shower shelves — get the wet stuff off the floor

Bottles standing in the shower tray are what makes a small bathroom feel

[adhesive or suction shower shelves](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=shower+shelf+adhesive+no+drill&tag=tamirugc20-20)

## How to buy for a small bathroom without wasting money

Measure three things before you order anything: the width of your narrowest
gap, the height under your window or light, and the thickness of your door.
Those three numbers rule out most of what's for sale, which is exactly what you
want them to do. Then buy in the order above — wall first, door second, under
the sink third. Almost everybody buys baskets first and wonders why the room
still feels full; baskets organise things that already have a home, they don't
create one.

## Common questions

### What is the best storage for a very small bathroom?

Start with the wall above the toilet. An
[over-the-toilet shelf unit](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=over+the+toilet+storage+shelf&tag=tamirugc20-20)
adds three shelves without taking a centimetre of floor, which is the constraint
that actually binds in a tiny bathroom. Do that before you buy a single basket.

### How do I add bathroom storage without drilling?

Use the door and the tiles. An
[over-the-door organizer](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=over+the+door+bathroom+organizer&tag=tamirugc20-20)
needs no fixings at all, and adhesive shower shelves hold well on smooth dry
tile if you leave them 24 hours before loading. Both come off cleanly, which
makes them the standard answer for renters.

### Where do you put towels in a small bathroom?

On a rail with two or three bars, rolled rather than folded — rolling fits more
in the same length and dries faster. A
[wall-mounted towel rack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=wall+mounted+towel+rack+bathroom&tag=tamirugc20-20)
on the back of the door works when there's no free wall left, and keeps the
towels off the radiator where they never quite dry.

### Are over-the-toilet shelves stable enough?

The straddling kind are, as long as you load the bottom shelf heaviest and
don't stand it on a bath mat, which lets the feet rock. If you have small
children or a very uneven floor, choose a model with a wall anchor strap —
most include one and it takes one screw.
