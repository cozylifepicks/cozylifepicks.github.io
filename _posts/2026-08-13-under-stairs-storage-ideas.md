## 1. Measure the triangle before you buy anything

Three numbers do it: the height at the tall end, the height at the low end, and
the depth from front to back. Write them on your phone. Almost every
under-stairs mistake is a bin that's two centimetres too tall, and the only way
to avoid it is to shop with the numbers in your hand rather than a picture in
your head.

## 2. Give the deep end wheels

The back of an under-stairs cupboard is the least accessible storage in the
house. Instead of crawling in, put things on a
[narrow rolling cart](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=narrow+rolling+storage+cart+slim&tag=tamirugc20-20)
and pull the whole back wall out when you need it. Slim ones are usually 15–20cm
wide and were designed for the gap beside a fridge, which is almost exactly the
shape you're dealing with.

## 3. Stack bins to follow the slope

This is the trick that makes the shape work for you instead of against you.
[Clear stackable bins](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=clear+stackable+storage+bins+with+lids&tag=tamirugc20-20)
go three high under the tall end, two high in the middle, one at the low end —
and suddenly the whole floor is used, not just the bit you can stand in. Clear
ones matter more here than anywhere else in the house, because you will never
pull a box out just to check.

→ [See current options on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=clear+stackable+storage+bins+with+lids&tag=tamirugc20-20)

## 4. Put a light in before you put anything else in

A cupboard you can't see into gets treated as a hole. A

## 5. Hang a rail in the tall section

The tall end is usually 150cm or more — enough for coats. A
[tension rod or short closet rail](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=adjustable+closet+tension+rod&tag=tamirugc20-20)
across the widest part turns it into a proper coat cupboard, which takes the
daily pressure off your entryway. If the walls are plasterboard, a tension rod
avoids drilling entirely.

## 6. Make the low end the shoe end

Shoes are short, and the low end is short. It's the one match the shape makes for
free. A
[slim two-tier shoe rack](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=slim+2+tier+shoe+rack&tag=tamirugc20-20)
slides in where nothing else fits, and it keeps the family's daily pairs out of
the hallway. If your under-stairs space opens into the hall, this idea alone
usually fixes the shoe pile — the same problem we solve from the other direction
in our [entryway ideas](/entryway-mudroom-ideas/).

## 7. Turn it into the cleaning cupboard

Vacuum, mop, broom, and the box of cloths under them. A
[wall-mounted broom and mop holder](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=wall+mounted+broom+and+mop+holder&tag=tamirugc20-20)
screwed to the side wall gets the handles off the floor, which is the only reason
cleaning cupboards ever look chaotic. Keep the vacuum nearest the door — it's the
thing you reach for most.

## 8. Store the seasonal stuff at the very back

Christmas decorations, the winter duvet, the camping gear: things you touch once
or twice a year belong in the least reachable spot. Pack soft items into
[vacuum storage bags](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=vacuum+storage+bags+for+bedding&tag=tamirugc20-20)
first — a compressed duvet takes about a third of the space, and the deep end is
exactly where that saving pays off.

## 9. Add a shelf at head height

Above the coats there's usually 30–40cm of dead air. One
[floating shelf](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=floating+wall+shelf+white&tag=tamirugc20-20)
across the tall end holds hats, gloves, the toolbox and the spare lightbulbs.
It's the cheapest capacity you'll add to the whole cupboard, and it stops the
floor becoming the only surface.

## 10. Baskets for anything soft

Hard bins are wrong for scarves, dog towels, hats and bags — they never fill
neatly. A row of
[woven storage baskets](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=woven+storage+baskets+with+handles&tag=tamirugc20-20)
on the shelf handles all of it, and if your under-stairs space is open to the
room rather than behind a door, they're the only option that looks like
furniture instead of storage.

## 11. Give the dog the low end

If the space is open-fronted, the short end makes a genuinely good dog nook: it's
enclosed on three sides, out of the walking line, and dogs like a low ceiling
more than we do. Add a [bed with a rim](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=donut+calming+dog+bed&tag=tamirugc20-20)
and it becomes the spot they choose — the same logic behind our

## 12. Or make it a reading nook instead

An open under-stairs space with a cushion, a small
[plug-in wall light](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=plug+in+wall+sconce+reading+light&tag=tamirugc20-20)
and a basket of books is one of the few corners in a small home that feels

## 13. Label the fronts, not the lids

Everything under the stairs gets stacked, which means lids are invisible.
Put the label on the **front face** of each bin with a
[label maker or write-on labels](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=write+on+storage+bin+labels&tag=tamirugc20-20),
facing out. Two minutes of labelling is the difference between a system that
survives a year and one that reverts to a pile by October.

## Common questions

### What can you store under the stairs?

Anything you don't need daily, plus the two things you do: coats and shoes. The
tall end suits a
[coat rail](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=adjustable+closet+tension+rod&tag=tamirugc20-20)
and a shelf, the middle suits cleaning kit and stacked bins, and the deep low end
suits seasonal boxes. Keep anything you use weekly within arm's reach of the
door.

### How do I add under-stairs storage without building anything?

Use freestanding pieces sized to the slope: a slim rolling cart, stackable clear
bins, a two-tier shoe rack and a tension rod. All four come out with you when you
move, which matters if you rent. Built-in drawers look better, but they cost
hundreds and need a carpenter.

### Is it safe to store things under the stairs?

Keep the space clear of the electricity meter, the fuse box and any boiler, leave
those accessible, and don't store fuel, paint thinner or gas canisters in an
enclosed cupboard. If your under-stairs cupboard is also your only route to a
stopcock or meter, storage goes around it, never in front of it.

### How much does under-stairs storage cost?

Doing it with freestanding pieces, most people spend the price of a
[rolling cart](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=narrow+rolling+storage+cart+slim&tag=tamirugc20-20),
a set of
[stackable bins](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=clear+stackable+storage+bins+with+lids&tag=tamirugc20-20)
and a light — a fraction of a fitted solution, and you can do it in an afternoon.
