## How to size it in a small room (30 seconds)

Measure your dog nose to tail-base while they're lying down, add about six
inches, and that's the *inside* of the bed you need — not the outside. Then
measure the actual gap in your room. If the two numbers fight, choose the
donut or the corner bed; both hold a curled dog in less floor than a rectangle
needs. And put the bed where your dog already sleeps by choice. A better bed in
the wrong place loses to a rug in the right one every single time.

## Common questions

### What size dog bed should I get for a small apartment?

Measure your dog lying down from nose to the base of the tail and add roughly
six inches — that's the internal size. In a small flat, prefer a
[donut bed](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=donut+calming+dog+bed+small&tag=tamirugc20-20)
one size down over a rectangle one size up: dogs sleep curled far more often
than stretched out, and the circle costs you much less floor.

### Where should a dog bed go in a small home?

Wherever your dog already naps — usually near the sofa, in sight of the door,
and out of the walking line between rooms. Corners and the gap beside a sofa
work best. Avoid directly under a radiator or in front of a heat vent; dogs
overheat on a bed they can't move away from.

### Are orthopedic dog beds worth it for a young dog?

For a young, light dog on carpet, a normal padded bed is fine. For a dog over
about seven, a heavy breed, or a home with hard floors,
[memory foam](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=orthopedic+memory+foam+dog+bed&tag=tamirugc20-20)
is the upgrade that matters most — it's the difference between resting and
lying on a floor with fabric over it.

### How often should you wash a dog bed?

Every one to two weeks in a small home, and immediately after anything muddy.
That's only realistic with a
[removable zip-off cover](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=washable+dog+bed+removable+cover&tag=tamirugc20-20),
so treat that as a requirement rather than a feature. A waterproof liner
underneath keeps the inner padding from ever needing a wash at all.
