## How to buy pantry organizers without wasting money

Measure two things before you order: **shelf depth** and **the height between
shelves**. Depth decides whether a bin can come out without catching the door
frame; height decides whether a riser or a stacked container fits at all. Almost
every returned pantry organizer failed on one of those two numbers.

Then buy in this order — bins first, riser second, containers third. Bins fix the
unreachable back half, which is the biggest problem in a small pantry. The riser
creates new space cheaply. Containers are the nicest-looking step and the one
worth doing last, because until the layout works, matching jars are just tidier
chaos.

One more rule: buy for the pantry you actually have. A set of twelve identical
containers photographs beautifully and rarely survives contact with a cupboard
that's 28cm deep.

## Common questions

### What is the best pantry organizer for a small kitchen?

A set of
[clear stackable bins with handles](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=clear+stackable+pantry+storage+bins&tag=tamirugc20-20).
In a small pantry the problem is almost never total volume, it's that the back
half of each shelf is unreachable — and a pull-out bin is the cheapest thing that
fixes that. Add an expandable riser next if your shelves are tall.

### How do I organize a small pantry with deep shelves?

Work in pull-out units rather than rows. Put one category per bin, keep the
things you use daily at the front in an open basket, and put a
[turntable](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=lazy+susan+turntable+organizer+pantry&tag=tamirugc20-20)
in the corner where bottles disappear. Deep shelves only work if everything on
them can come toward you.

### Are airtight containers worth it for a pantry?

For flour, rice, pasta, cereal and anything that comes in a bag, yes — they stack,
they don't spill, and they show you what's left before you buy a fourth bag of
lentils. For tins and jars they change nothing, so don't decant those. Square
shapes beat round ones in a small cupboard.

### How do I stop my pantry getting messy again?

Give every category a fixed home and label it, so putting things away needs no
decisions. The two habits that keep it are unpacking the shopping into the bins
rather than onto the shelf, and doing a two-minute front-to-back pull once a
month. Our
[15 pantry organization ideas](/pantry-organization-ideas/) go through the
routine in more detail.
